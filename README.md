1. Introduction
Maternal health plays a vital role in ensuring the well-being of both the mother and the baby during
pregnancy. Early identification of maternal health risks can help healthcare professionals provide
timely treatment and reduce pregnancy-related complications. Machine learning techniques have
become an effective solution for analyzing healthcare data and supporting accurate medical decisionmaking.
The project "Maternal Risk Prediction using Machine Learning Model" aims to develop an intelligent
system that predicts the maternal health risk level using important health parameters such as Age,
Systolic Blood Pressure, Diastolic Blood Pressure, Blood Sugar (BS), Body Temperature, and Heart
Rate. In this project, a Random Forest Classifier is used as the machine learning algorithm to analyze
the maternal health dataset and classify patients into Low Risk, Mid Risk, and High Risk categories.
The system is developed using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Flask, HTML, and CSS. The
trained machine learning model is integrated with a Flask-based web application to provide fast and
reliable risk predictions. This system assists healthcare professionals in the early detection of maternal
health risks, supports better clinical decision-making, and contributes to improving maternal
healthcare management.
Proposal & Problem Definition
3.1 Proposal:
The Maternal Health Risk Prediction System is a machine learning-based application developed to
predict the health risk level of pregnant women. The system uses parameters such as Age, Blood
Pressure, Blood Sugar, Body Temperature, and Heart Rate to classify patients into Low Risk, Mid
Risk, or High Risk categories. This helps in early detection and timely medical intervention.
3.2 Problem Definition
Pregnant women are vulnerable to various health complications that can adversely affect maternal
and fetal health. Identifying high-risk pregnancies at an early stage is crucial for ensuring proper
medical care and reducing complications. However, manual assessment methods may not always be
efficient, especially when dealing with large numbers of patients.
The main problem addressed in this project is the development of an intelligent system capable of
accurately predicting maternal health risks using patient health data. By applying machine learning
algorithms to historical maternal health records, the system can assist healthcare professionals in
making faster and more informed decisions. The proposed solution aims to improve maternal
healthcare management through automated, accurate, and data-driven risk prediction.
3.3 Problem Identification
Maternal health complications during pregnancy can pose serious risks to both the mother and the
baby. Factors such as high blood pressure, abnormal blood sugar levels, body temperature, and heart
rate can increase the chances of health problems if not identified early. Traditional risk assessment
methods rely on manual evaluation, which can be time-consuming and may not always provide
accurate predictions. Therefore, there is a need for an automated system that can analyze maternal
health data and predict risk levels effectively. This project aims to develop a machine learning-based
system that classifies pregnant women into Low Risk, Mid Risk, and High Risk categories, enabling
timely medical intervention and better healthcare decisions.
4.4 Methodology
The methodology describes the step-by-step process followed to develop the Maternal Risk
Prediction System. It begins with collecting and preprocessing maternal health data, followed by
feature selection and machine learning model development using the Random Forest Classifier. The
model is then trained, tested, and integrated into a Flask-based web application to generate maternal health risk predictions. The complete workflow ensures accurate and efficient classification of
patients into Low Risk, Mid Risk, and High Risk categories.
